What customers say

Questions

Does my phone need to be on?

No. Once your number is linked, posts are sent for you. Your phone only needs to come online now and then so WhatsApp keeps the link active.

Which number do posts come from?

Your own. You link your WhatsApp the same way you link WhatsApp Web, and you can unlink it at any time from your phone or from here.

Can I post to channels?

Yes, to channels your number owns or is an admin of, and to groups your number is a member of.

What happens if a post fails?

It retries every five minutes for a day. Places it already reached are remembered, so nothing is sent twice.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account page. You keep what you paid for until the end of the period.
